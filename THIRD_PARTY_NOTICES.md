## Apple platforms

The iOS touch runner (`native/ios-runner`) is built with Xcode on the user's
computer and uses private XCTest interfaces (event synthesis through the
XCTest daemon) and Apple's undocumented CoreMediaIO screen-capture device
switch. These are not part of any public Apple SDK and may change with new
Xcode or iOS releases.
